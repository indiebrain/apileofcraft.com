#+TITLE: About
#+DESCRIPTION: What this site is and who it is for.

*A Pile of Craft* is a record of the things the people on one street make with
their hands — birdhouses, bread, quilts, jam, furniture, zines, whatever turns
up on a workbench or a kitchen counter.

The rule is simple: it has to be made by a neighbor, and it has to be real. No
kits pretending to be craft, no reselling. Just the stuff that gets made a few
doors down and would otherwise go unrecorded.

If you live on the street and you made a thing, it belongs here.
